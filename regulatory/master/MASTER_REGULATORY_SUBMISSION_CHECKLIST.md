## 3. Critical Path Analysis

The following dependency chain governs the overall timeline. Activities on the critical path cannot be parallelized and directly determine the earliest possible market entry date.

```
Month 0–3:   Foundation work (parallel for all devices)
             ├── ISO 13485 QMS implementation
             ├── Risk management files (ISO 14971)
             ├── IEC 62304 software documentation
             └── Cybersecurity documentation

Month 3–9:   Testing and clinical (parallel tracks)
             ├── IEC 60601-1 safety testing (12–18 weeks)
             ├── IEC 60601-1-2 EMC testing (8–12 weeks)
             ├── FCC authorization (6–10 weeks)
             └── Clinical studies begin (IRB approval + enrollment)

Month 6–12:  HEALTH-KEY ULTRA 510(k) preparation
             ├── Biocompatibility testing (ISO 10993) — 8–12 weeks
             ├── Usability validation (IEC 60601-1-6) — 8–12 weeks
             └── 510(k) package assembly

Month 12–18: HEALTH-KEY ULTRA 510(k) submission + FDA review
             ├── FDA review period: 90 days (standard) or 30 days (expedited)
             └── Respond to FDA additional information requests

Month 18–24: HEALTH-KEY ULTRA market launch
             └── HEALTH-BAND Neuro 510(k) submission (parallel track)

Month 24–36: HEALTH-RING + HEALTH-LAB De Novo submissions
             └── Requires clinical study completion (18–24 months from start)
```

### Critical Path Items (cannot be delayed without pushing market entry)

1. **ISO 13485 QMS implementation** — Must be in place before any testing begins
2. **IEC 60601-1 safety testing** — Required for FDA submission; 12–18 week lead time
3. **Clinical studies** — Required for De Novo devices; 18–24 month lead time
4. **FCC authorization** — Required before any commercial sale; 6–10 week lead time
5. **Biocompatibility testing** — Required for FDA submission; 8–12 week lead time
