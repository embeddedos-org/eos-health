## 1. Labeling Requirements Overview

FDA requires that all medical device labeling comply with 21 CFR Part 801. For 510(k) submissions, labeling must be included per 21 CFR 807.87(e). Key requirements:

- Labeling must not be false or misleading (21 CFR 801.6)
- Adequate directions for use must be provided (21 CFR 801.5)
- Warnings, precautions, and contraindications must be prominent
- Intended use must match the 510(k) cleared indication
- UDI (Unique Device Identifier) required per 21 CFR Part 830

### 2.2 Intended Use Statement (for FDA Submission)

The EoS Health HEALTH-KEY ULTRA is intended for use as a general wellness monitoring device for adults 18 years of age and older. The device monitors heart rate, blood oxygen saturation (SpO₂), single-lead electrocardiogram (ECG), and transdermal alcohol content (BAC). The device is intended for use in home and non-clinical settings. The device is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

### 2.3 Indications for Use (for FDA Submission)

The HEALTH-KEY ULTRA is indicated for:
1. Continuous and spot-check monitoring of heart rate in adults ≥18 years
2. Spot-check monitoring of blood oxygen saturation (SpO₂) in adults ≥18 years with SpO₂ ≥70%
3. Recording of single-lead ECG for detection of atrial fibrillation (AFib) in adults ≥18 years
4. Monitoring of transdermal alcohol content as a surrogate for blood alcohol content in adults ≥21 years

**Contraindications:**
- Not for use in patients with implanted cardiac devices (pacemakers, ICDs) — ECG electrodes may interfere
- Not for use during MRI
- Not for use as a substitute for clinical ECG or pulse oximetry
- Not for use in pediatric patients (<18 years)

### 2.4 Instructions for Use (IFU) — Key Sections

**Section 1: Intended Use**
The HEALTH-KEY ULTRA is a wellness monitoring device. It is NOT a medical device and is NOT intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice.

**Section 2: Warnings**
⚠️ WARNING: Do not use if you have an implanted cardiac device (pacemaker, ICD, CRT). The ECG electrodes may interfere with your device.  
⚠️ WARNING: Do not use as a substitute for clinical pulse oximetry. If you experience symptoms of hypoxia (confusion, shortness of breath, blue lips), seek emergency medical care immediately.  
⚠️ WARNING: Do not use during MRI. The device contains metal components and a battery.  
⚠️ WARNING: The BAC reading is an estimate only. Do not use to determine fitness to drive. Legal BAC limits vary by jurisdiction.  
⚠️ WARNING: Keep away from children under 12. Small parts present choking hazard.

**Section 3: Precautions**
- Remove device before swimming in saltwater (IP68 rated for freshwater only)
- Do not charge via damaged USB cables
- If skin irritation develops, discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional
- Accuracy may be reduced in users with dark skin pigmentation, nail polish, or poor peripheral circulation

**Section 4: How to Use**
1. Insert HEALTH-KEY ULTRA into USB-C port of your smartphone
2. Open the EoS Health app and follow pairing instructions
3. For ECG: Place fingertips on the two ECG contact pads for 30 seconds
4. For SpO₂: Place fingertip over the PPG sensor window for 10 seconds
5. For BAC: Breathe normally near the sensor for 60 seconds
6. View results in the EoS Health app

**Section 5: Cleaning and Maintenance**
- Wipe with 70% isopropyl alcohol wipe after each use
- Do not submerge in cleaning solutions
- Do not use abrasive cleaners

## 3. HEALTH-BAND Neuro — Labeling

### 3.1 Intended Use Statement

The EoS Health HEALTH-BAND Neuro is intended for use as a general wellness monitoring and therapy device for adults 18 years of age and older. The device monitors surface electromyography (sEMG), electrodermal activity (EDA), heart rate, and ECG, and delivers transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation (TENS) for temporary relief of minor muscle pain. The device is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

### 3.2 Indications for Use

1. Monitoring of surface electromyography (sEMG) for gesture recognition and muscle activity monitoring in adults ≥18 years
2. Monitoring of electrodermal activity (EDA) as a general wellness indicator in adults ≥18 years
3. Delivery of TENS for temporary relief of minor muscle and joint pain in adults ≥18 years
4. Monitoring of heart rate and single-lead ECG in adults ≥18 years

### 3.3 Contraindications

⚠️ **CONTRAINDICATIONS — Do NOT use if you have:**
- Implanted electronic device (pacemaker, ICD, cochlear implant, drug infusion pump)
- Epilepsy or seizure disorder
- Pregnancy
- Cancer in the treatment area
- Active deep vein thrombosis (DVT)
- Impaired sensation in the treatment area (diabetic neuropathy)
- Open wounds, rashes, or skin infections in the treatment area
- Age under 18 years

### 3.4 TENS Warnings (Required by IEC 60601-2-10 and FDA)

⚠️ WARNING: Do not apply TENS electrodes across the chest or near the heart.  
⚠️ WARNING: Do not apply TENS electrodes to the head, neck, or face.  
⚠️ WARNING: Do not use TENS while driving, operating machinery, or sleeping.  
⚠️ WARNING: Do not use TENS in water or while bathing.  
⚠️ WARNING: Do not use TENS if you have a cardiac arrhythmia.  
⚠️ WARNING: If you experience chest pain, shortness of breath, or cardiac symptoms during TENS use, stop immediately and seek emergency medical care.  
⚠️ WARNING: TENS is for temporary pain relief only. It is not a treatment for the underlying cause of pain. Consult a healthcare professional if pain persists.

## 4. HEALTH-RING — Labeling

### 4.1 Intended Use Statement

The EoS Health HEALTH-RING is intended for use as a general wellness monitoring device for adults 18 years of age and older. The device monitors heart rate, blood oxygen saturation (SpO₂), single-lead ECG, estimated HbA1c, estimated blood pressure, heart rate variability (HRV), and sleep stages. The device is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

### 4.2 Indications for Use

1. Continuous monitoring of heart rate in adults ≥18 years
2. Spot-check monitoring of SpO₂ in adults ≥18 years
3. Recording of single-lead ECG for detection of atrial fibrillation in adults ≥18 years
4. Estimation of HbA1c as a general wellness indicator in adults ≥18 years (not for diabetes diagnosis or management)
5. Estimation of blood pressure as a general wellness indicator in adults ≥18 years (not for hypertension diagnosis or management)
6. Monitoring of HRV and sleep stages as general wellness indicators

### 4.3 Critical Disclaimers (Required for FTC + FDA Compliance)

⚠️ **IMPORTANT: HbA1c and Blood Pressure Readings**  
The HEALTH-RING HbA1c and blood pressure readings are **estimates for general wellness monitoring only**. They are **NOT** intended to diagnose diabetes, hypertension, or any other medical condition. They are **NOT** a substitute for laboratory HbA1c testing or clinical blood pressure measurement. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical diagnosis and treatment decisions.

⚠️ **IMPORTANT: AFib Detection**  
The HEALTH-RING AFib detection feature is intended to detect possible atrial fibrillation. A positive result does **NOT** confirm a diagnosis of AFib. A negative result does **NOT** rule out AFib or other cardiac conditions. Always consult a healthcare professional for any cardiac concerns.

## 5. HEALTH-LAB — Labeling

### 5.1 Intended Use Statement

The EoS Health HEALTH-LAB is intended for use as a general wellness monitoring device for adults 18 years of age and older. The device monitors sweat glucose, lactate, cortisol, sodium, potassium, uric acid, and pH as general wellness indicators. The device is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

### 5.2 Indications for Use

1. Monitoring of sweat glucose as a general wellness indicator in adults ≥18 years (not for diabetes diagnosis or management)
2. Monitoring of sweat lactate as a general wellness indicator for exercise performance in adults ≥18 years
3. Monitoring of sweat cortisol as a general wellness indicator for stress monitoring in adults ≥18 years
4. Monitoring of sweat electrolytes (Na⁺, K⁺) as general wellness indicators for hydration monitoring

### 5.3 Critical Disclaimers

⚠️ **IMPORTANT: Sweat Glucose Readings**  
The HEALTH-LAB sweat glucose readings are **estimates for general wellness monitoring only**. Sweat glucose correlates with blood glucose but is **NOT** equivalent to blood glucose. The HEALTH-LAB is **NOT** a blood glucose monitor and is **NOT** intended for diabetes management. Always use a FDA-cleared blood glucose monitor for diabetes management decisions. Always consult a healthcare professional.

### 5.4 Adhesive Warnings

⚠️ WARNING: If skin redness, rash, or irritation develops, remove the patch immediately and consult a healthcare professional.  
⚠️ WARNING: Do not apply to broken, irritated, or sunburned skin.  
⚠️ WARNING: Do not apply to areas with hair without first shaving.  
⚠️ WARNING: Replace patch every 14 days maximum. Do not reuse patches.

## 7. FCC Labeling Requirements

Per 47 CFR §15.19, all FCC-authorized devices must display the FCC ID on the label.

**Required FCC label text:**
```
FCC ID: [FCC ID assigned by TCB]
This device complies with Part 15 of the FCC Rules. Operation is subject to the following two conditions:
(1) This device may not cause harmful interference, and
(2) this device must accept any interference received, including interference that may cause undesired operation.
```

**For devices where FCC ID cannot fit on label** (HEALTH-RING — small form factor):
Per 47 CFR §15.19(a)(7), the FCC ID may be displayed electronically (e-label) in the device's associated app.
