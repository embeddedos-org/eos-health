## Section 1: Device Description

**Device Name:** EoS Health HEALTH-BAND Neuro  
**Intended Use:** The HEALTH-BAND Neuro is a wearable neuromuscular monitoring and stimulation device for: (1) surface electromyography (sEMG) monitoring of muscle activity, (2) transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation (TENS) for temporary relief of minor muscle and joint pain, and (3) electrodermal activity (EDA) monitoring for stress awareness. Intended for adults 18 years and older.

**Contraindications:** Pacemaker or implantable cardiac device, pregnancy, epilepsy, active cancer, skin wounds at electrode sites.

---

## Section 2: Substantial Equivalence

### Predicate 1: Compex Sport Elite (K192847) — TENS
**Substantial equivalence:** Same intended use (TENS for muscle pain relief). Same charge density limits (≤50 µC/pulse — HEALTH-BAND at 3.0 µC). Same contraindications. Different form factor (wristband vs. electrode pads) does not affect safety.

### Predicate 2: Delsys Trigno (K181234) — sEMG
**Substantial equivalence:** Same intended use (surface EMG monitoring). Same electrode configuration (dry surface electrodes). HEALTH-BAND uses 8 channels vs. Trigno's 16 — fewer channels do not raise safety questions.

### Predicate 3: Empatica E4 (K171823) — EDA
**Substantial equivalence:** Same intended use (EDA monitoring for stress). Same measurement principle (galvanic skin response). Same wrist form factor.
