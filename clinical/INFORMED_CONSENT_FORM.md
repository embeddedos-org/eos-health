## INTRODUCTION

Please take time to read the following information carefully and discuss it with others if you wish. Ask us if there is anything that is not clear or if you would like more information.

---

## PURPOSE OF THE STUDY

EoS Health is developing wearable health monitoring devices that can measure health indicators such as heart rhythm, blood oxygen, blood sugar (glucose), and muscle activity without needles or blood draws. We need to compare the readings from our devices with standard medical measurements to confirm they are accurate.

This study will involve wearing one or more EoS Health devices for 7 days while we compare their readings to standard medical tests.

---

## WHAT WILL HAPPEN IF YOU TAKE PART

**Screening Visit (1–2 hours):**
- We will review your medical history and check you are eligible
- You will have a physical examination
- We will take blood samples for standard laboratory tests
- We will fit the devices and show you how to use the smartphone app

**7-Day Monitoring Period:**
- You will wear the assigned device(s) continuously for 7 days
- You will visit the clinic on Days 2, 4, and 7 (approximately 1–2 hours each)
- At each clinic visit, we will take standard medical measurements alongside the device readings
- You will keep a simple diary of any discomfort or skin reactions

**Day 7 Final Visit (2–3 hours):**
- We will remove the devices and inspect your skin
- We will take final blood samples
- You will complete a comfort questionnaire

**Total time commitment:** Approximately 10–12 hours over 7 days, plus 3 clinic visits.

---

## POSSIBLE RISKS AND DISCOMFORTS

| Risk | Likelihood | Management |
|---|---|---|
| Mild skin irritation from adhesive patch (HEALTH-LAB) | 5–15% of participants | Remove patch, apply mild topical cream |
| Minor discomfort from ring wear | 5–10% | Adjust ring size or discontinue |
| Minor bruising from blood draws | Common | Standard care |
| Mild electrical sensation from TENS (HEALTH-BAND only) | Expected | Adjust intensity or stop |
| BLE signal interference with other devices | Very rare | No clinical significance |

There are **no X-rays, no ionizing radiation, and no experimental medications** in this study.

---

## POSSIBLE BENEFITS

You may benefit from:
- 7 days of comprehensive health monitoring at no cost to you
- Early detection of undiagnosed heart rhythm problems (atrial fibrillation)
- Detailed blood sugar trends (if diabetic)
- A copy of your study health data

There is no guarantee that you will personally benefit from taking part. However, the information gathered may help develop better health monitoring tools for everyone.

---

## CONFIDENTIALITY

All information collected about you during this study will be kept strictly confidential. Your name will not appear in any publications or reports. You will be identified only by a participant ID number.

Your data will be:
- Stored on encrypted servers (AES-256 encryption)
- Accessible only to authorized study staff
- Retained for 15 years as required by law
- Potentially shared in anonymized form with other researchers

Your data will **not** be sold to third parties or used for advertising.
