### EoS Foundation Files Two USPTO Patents in Five Days for Next-Generation Wearable Health Devices

**Santa Clara, CA — May 28, 2026** — The Embedded Operating Systems Research Foundation (EoS Foundation), a 501(c)(3) non-profit research organization, today announced the filing of two USPTO provisional patent applications for next-generation wearable health monitoring devices: **HEALTH-KEY ULTRA** (U.S. App. No. 64/073,334, filed May 23, 2026) and **HEALTH-BAND Neuro** (U.S. App. No. 64/076,078, filed May 27, 2026).

Both devices were invented by Srikanth Patchava, Founder of the EoS Foundation, and introduce three hardware architectures with no prior art in any commercial wearable device currently on the market.

**HEALTH-KEY ULTRA** is a USB-C health key that uses a patented Zero-Hole Architecture (ZHA) — the USB-C connector itself serves as the only opening in the device, eliminating the need for a dedicated charging port while delivering 15W pass-through power delivery, USB 2.0 data, and ECG signal routing. The device integrates heart rate, SpO₂, skin temperature, UV index, and a 64 GB encrypted health data vault.

**HEALTH-BAND Neuro** is a wrist-worn health monitoring band that combines three novel technologies: the same Zero-Hole Architecture as HEALTH-KEY ULTRA; a Bidirectional Neuromuscular Electrode Array (BNEA) that alternates between surface electromyography (sEMG) gesture sensing and transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation (TENS) therapy on the same six platinum electrodes; and an Integrated Breath Analysis Channel (IBAC) that concentrates exhaled breath through a Venturi microchannel for real-time, non-invasive blood alcohol content (BAC) and VOC biomarker analysis.

"These devices represent a fundamental rethinking of how wearable health technology is designed," said Srikanth Patchava, inventor and Founder of the EoS Foundation. "The Zero-Hole Architecture solves the waterproofing-versus-convenience tradeoff that has plagued every wearable device since the original Apple Watch. The BNEA eliminates the need for two separate devices for gesture sensing and therapy. The IBAC brings breath-based diagnostics to the wrist for the first time."

Both devices are supported by the **EoS Health** companion application — a full-stack web platform built on React 19, Express 4, and tRPC 11 — available at https://github.com/embeddedos-org. The firmware runs on **EmbeddedOS**, the Foundation's own real-time operating system for the nRF52840 microcontroller.

The Foundation plans to file non-provisional patent applications for both devices before their respective 12-month deadlines (May 23, 2027 and May 27, 2027) and is actively seeking collaboration with clinical researchers, hardware manufacturers, and digital health investors.

**About the EoS Foundation**
The Embedded Operating Systems Research Foundation is a 501(c)(3) non-profit research organization (EIN: 41-4821627) based in Santa Clara, California. The Foundation develops open-source embedded operating systems, health monitoring devices, and digital health applications. Its work is publicly available at https://embeddedos-org.github.io and https://github.com/embeddedos-org.

- **HEALTH-BAND Neuro** — U.S. App. No. 64/076,078 (filed May 27, 2026)

Both devices introduce a patented **Zero-Hole Architecture** — the USB-C connector is the only opening in the device. No dedicated charging port. No water ingress point. The clasp itself charges the device at 15W and routes ECG signals. No Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin, or Samsung device does this.

The HEALTH-BAND Neuro goes further with two additional patented innovations:

**Bidirectional Neuromuscular Electrode Array (BNEA):** Six platinum electrodes on the inner strap surface alternate between reading muscle signals (sEMG, 16 gestures, 94% accuracy) and delivering therapeutic electrical stimulation (TENS, 5 clinical protocols). One device. Two functions. No prior art.

**Integrated Breath Analysis Channel (IBAC):** A Venturi microchannel in the USB-C latch housing concentrates your exhaled breath onto a fuel cell BAC sensor and a VOC biomarker sensor. Real-time breath alcohol and metabolic health from your wrist. No mouthpiece. No prior art.
