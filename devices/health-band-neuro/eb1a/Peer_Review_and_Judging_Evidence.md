## How to Document Judging Activity for USCIS

For each peer review completed, collect and preserve:

1. **Invitation email** from the journal/conference editor requesting the review
2. **Confirmation email** acknowledging review submission
3. **Review system screenshot** showing the submission reviewed and the review completed
4. **Letter from editor** confirming participation as a reviewer (request this after 2–3 reviews)

These documents should be organized in `eb1a/evidence/criterion-4/` with filenames like:
- `techrxiv_review_invitation_2026_07.pdf`
- `ieee_embc_reviewer_confirmation_2026_09.pdf`
- `sensors_mdpi_review_completion_2026_08.pdf`
