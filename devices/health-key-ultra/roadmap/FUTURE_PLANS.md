# HEALTH-KEY Ecosystem: Future Roadmap & Patent Strategy

This document outlines the strategic product roadmap and the Continuation-In-Part (CIP) patent filing plan for the HEALTH-KEY and HEALTH-BAND Neuro ecosystem.

## 1. Product Roadmap

### Phase 1: HEALTH-KEY Pro (V1) - Consumer Launch
* **Target:** Q1 2027
* **Market:** General consumer, fitness, driver safety.
* **Regulatory:** FCC Part 15, CE Mark, RoHS (No FDA required).
* **Key Features:** 6 sensors, BLE + USB-C HID, basic BAC via Dart fuel cell.
* **Price Point:** $149–$199

### Phase 2: HEALTH-KEY ULTRA (V2) - Clinical Launch
* **Target:** Q4 2027
* **Market:** Hospitals, clinics, workplace safety, professional athletics.
* **Regulatory:** FDA 510(k) Class II, IEEE 11073, ISO 80601.
* **Key Features:** 9 sensors, permanent PTFE Venturi channel, MAX30101 dual-purpose LED, clinical-grade accuracy.
* **Price Point:** $299–$499

### Phase 3: HEALTH-BAND Neuro - Wearable Therapeutics
* **Target:** Q2 2028
* **Market:** Neurological rehabilitation, advanced HCI (Human-Computer Interaction), continuous monitoring.
* **Regulatory:** FDA 510(k) for TENS/tDCS.
* **Key Features:** Zero-hole architecture, sEMG gesture control, neuromodulation, pass-through charging.
* **Price Point:** $399–$499

### Phase 4: HEALTH-KEY MAX (V3) - Portable Laboratory
* **Target:** 2029+
* **Market:** Medical research, point-of-care diagnostics.
* **Regulatory:** FDA Class III PMA, CE IVD.
* **Key Features:** Breathomics panel (selective VOC array), microfluidic saliva diagnostics via disposable cartridges.

## 2. Patent Strategy: Continuation-In-Part (CIP)

The initial Provisional Patent Application (US App. No. 64/073,334) filed on May 23, 2026, covers the core HEALTH-KEY ULTRA architecture. To protect the expanded vision, a Continuation-In-Part (CIP) or a new provisional application must be filed.

### Core Claims to Add/Expand:

1.  **The Zero-Hole Architecture:** A wearable device comprising exactly one USB-C Male plug and one USB-C Female port, wherein the two ports connect to form a closed loop, and wherein the Female port also serves as a breath inlet for an internal Venturi channel.
2.  **Pass-Through Health Monitoring:** A method of performing continuous health monitoring while a host device is charging, comprising an inline USB-C module that passes power to the host while actively sampling environmental and physiological sensors.
3.  **Dual-Purpose Optical Biosensor:** The use of a multi-wavelength PPG LED array (e.g., MAX30101) where the visible light channels (Green/Red) are actively modulated to indicate device status (charging, pairing, readiness) between physiological sampling intervals.
4.  **Integrated Hydrophobic Breath Channel:** A recessed breath inlet positioned on the device body surface, separated from the electrical connector face, comprising a permanently integrated hydrophobic PTFE membrane to block liquid ingress while permitting volatile organic compounds to reach an internal sensor array.
5.  **Modular sEMG/Neuromodulation Strap:** A flexible wrist strap comprising surface electromyography (sEMG) and neuromodulation electrodes, powered and controlled by a detachable rigid core module.

* **Deadline:** The Non-Provisional application based on the original 64/073,334 filing is due by **May 23, 2027**.
* **Action:** Draft the CIP claims incorporating the HEALTH-BAND Neuro and the refined V2/V3 features, and file concurrently with or prior to the Non-Provisional deadline.
