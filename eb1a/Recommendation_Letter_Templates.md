## EB-1A Extraordinary Ability Petition — Expert Letters

**Petitioner:** Srikanth Patchava | EoS Foundation
**Patent Pending:** U.S. App. Nos. 64/073,334 and 64/076,078

---

## Overview

USCIS requires expert recommendation letters from recognized authorities in the petitioner's field. For an EB-1A petition, 5–8 letters are recommended. Letters should come from experts who can speak to the significance of the petitioner's contributions — ideally people who have NOT collaborated directly with the petitioner (independent experts carry more weight than colleagues or co-authors).

Each letter should:
- Be on official institutional letterhead
- Include the expert's credentials and recognition in the field
- Explain the significance of the petitioner's specific contributions
- State that the contributions are original and of major significance
- Be signed with a wet or digital signature

## Instructions for Obtaining Letters

1. **Identify experts** — Search Google Scholar, IEEE Xplore, and PubMed for researchers who have published in the specific areas (sEMG, TENS, wearable BAC, waterproof wearables, ZHA-adjacent topics)
2. **Send a brief introduction email** — Introduce yourself, describe your work, attach the Zenodo record and one-page summary, and ask if they would be willing to write a letter
3. **Provide the template** — Share the relevant template above and offer to provide any additional technical information they need
4. **Follow up** — Allow 2–3 weeks for a response; follow up once if no response
5. **Collect on official letterhead** — The letter must be on the expert's institutional letterhead with their signature

**Target: 5–8 letters from independent experts (not collaborators or colleagues)**

---

*For EB-1A purposes: Expert recommendation letters from recognized authorities in the field are among the most persuasive evidence for the final merits determination (Step 2 of the USCIS two-step analysis).*
