# DOCUMENT 6: DATA RETENTION AND DELETION POLICY

**EoS Health Data Retention and Deletion Policy**  
Effective Date: [INSERT]

## 1. Retention Schedule

| Data Category | Retention Period | Legal Basis |
|---------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Account profile | Account lifetime + 30 days post-deletion | Contract |
| Health sensor data (general) | 7 years from collection | HIPAA §164.530(j) |
| ECG recordings | 10 years from collection | Clinical standard |
| Clinical study data | 15 years from study completion | FDA 21 CFR 812.140 |
| Adverse event reports | 15 years | FDA 21 CFR 803 |
| Payment records | 7 years | IRS/tax law |
| Crash/diagnostic logs | 90 days | Operational need |
| Marketing analytics | 2 years | Legitimate interest |
| Support tickets | 3 years | Legitimate interest |

## 2. Deletion Process

**User-initiated deletion:** Users may delete their account via Settings → Account → Delete Account. This initiates a 30-day grace period during which the account can be restored. After 30 days, all personal data is permanently deleted from production systems. Backups are purged within 90 days.

**Exceptions to deletion:** Data subject to legal hold, regulatory investigation, or mandatory retention periods (listed above) cannot be deleted until the retention period expires.

## 3. Data Minimization

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide the Service. Sensor data is processed on-device where possible. Raw sensor data is not transmitted to the cloud unless the user explicitly enables cloud backup.

## 4. Anonymization

After the applicable retention period, data that cannot be deleted (e.g., for research purposes) is irreversibly anonymized using k-anonymity (k≥5) and differential privacy techniques before any further use.

## 5. Breach Response

In the event of a data breach: (a) we will notify affected users within 72 hours of discovery; (b) we will notify the HHS Office for Civil Rights (OCR) within 60 days (HIPAA); (c) we will notify EU supervisory authorities within 72 hours (GDPR); (d) we will provide free credit monitoring for 12 months if financial data is involved.
